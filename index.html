About

An engineer's way of thinking, applied to data.

I started in civil engineering at Addis Ababa University, designing apartment buildings and pedestrian bridges in ETABS and SAP2000 and supervising construction sites.

Later I did research on natural-fibre composite materials at the University of Stuttgart. Somewhere along the way I noticed that the part I liked most was the data: test results, pricing analysis, site records. So I did an MSc in Data Analytics in Berlin, with a thesis on optimising CNN architectures for brain tumour segmentation.

Now I teach data science, databases and data visualisation at SRH University Berlin, and I build analytics systems: pipelines on BigQuery and dbt, ERP analytics, and RAG-based assistants. I'm especially interested in data for construction and for health care.
